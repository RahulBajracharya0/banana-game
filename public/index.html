How to play
  1. Type the digit each banana stands for.
  2. Correct answers earn 10 points, plus a streak bonus.
  3. A round is 10 puzzles or 3 wrong answers, whichever comes first.
  4. Hints cost 2 points each (3 per game).
  5. When the game ends, your points are saved. The leaderboard shows each player's best game.
